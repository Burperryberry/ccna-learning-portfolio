# Packet Tracer Lab Reflection Template

Copy this file into a lab-specific note after completing a Packet Tracer exercise. Use a filename such as `Day 28 - OSPF Part 3.md` and replace the prompts with your own experience.

## Lab objective

Briefly explain what you were asked to configure or troubleshoot.

## Configuration practiced

- List the important Cisco IOS commands or networking concepts you used.
- Mention any topology decisions that affected the solution.

## Problem encountered

Describe one mistake, unexpected result, or troubleshooting step. If the lab went smoothly, note the check that gave you the most confidence.

## Verification

Record the commands or tests that proved the configuration worked, such as `show` commands, successful pings, neighbor relationships, or spanning-tree state.

## What I learned

Summarize the main lesson in one or two sentences. Focus on what you would remember or do differently in a real network.
